Help & FAQ

Is LabourConnect free to use?

Yes. Registering, browsing and posting jobs are free.

How do I hire a worker?

Browse Find Workers, open a profile and tap Contact. You can also post a job and let workers apply.

How do workers find jobs?

Register as a worker, browse Find Jobs and tap Apply or call the employer directly.

Are workers verified?

Profiles show ratings and experience. Always confirm details and agree on pay before work begins.
